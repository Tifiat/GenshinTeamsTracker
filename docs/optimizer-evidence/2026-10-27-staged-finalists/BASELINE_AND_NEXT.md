## Protection on other teams

Two distinct risks need separate evidence. Pilot sampling can miss a contender
already in the nine-candidate panel; R2 promotion/reconsideration addresses this
heuristically. Formula error can prevent a good build entering that panel at all;
increasing final iterations cannot repair that. Neither protection is a universal
no-loss guarantee, especially for rare outcomes and poorly sampled variance.

Keep the measured incumbent available; validate useful predicted gains/ranking
against actual simulation, including feasible uncertain challengers within a
bounded existing budget. A common absolute DPS bias alone does not trigger repair.
On material ranking failure, preserve the best feasible measured build and
diagnose which contribution/context failed before spending more work. Any extra
budget/trace must be explicit and bounded; an unknown operation or changed set
alone does not authorize another capture. Do not silently turn unknowns into zero
or pruning certificates. Failure to improve must not discard a usable incumbent.

After a useful Chasca improvement, proposed expansion is Navia, then retained
cases with prior search failure/slowness, one at a time on full inventory. Before
using any case, pin its rotation/input and historical comparison; unknown old
timing stays unknown. First material anomaly holds the sequence. Only a diagnostic
that distinguishes competing causes may continue by explicit ROOT decision.
After an optimizer repair restart the admitted comparison sequence; old failed
rows remain evidence. No short wall timeout masquerading as a quality criterion.

## Deferred research: learn a local frozen residual from simulation

Required investigation item, not implemented by R2. Reuse existing paired-seed
measurements first. Compare **changes** predicted by the known formula with actual
changes: residual delta = delta(simulator DPS) - delta(known formula DPS).
Test whether a small bounded model of relevant actor/stat deltas can improve
ranking locally, without reconstructing the whole formula or adding a trace.

Total team DPS alone generally does not identify which frozen mechanic caused a
residual. Attribute to an individual frozen contribution only with engine-owned
component evidence or identifiable controlled contrasts. Otherwise label it an
aggregate contextual residual. Do not invent causal edges, count the known stat
effect twice, or treat correlation as parsed engine semantics. Graph dependencies
can select probe axes, but missing graph edges cannot prove independence.

Use a small dimension/range/call budget, noise-aware paired comparisons, and
held-out real artifact replacements, including losses in other stats. Test caps,
thresholds, interactions, rarity and transfer outside the fitted range. A learned
residual is an uncertain ranking guide, never a safe pruning/optimality bound.
Accept only if it improves whole-search quality/time on held-out cases. On failure
discard the correction and keep the measured incumbent; do not start an automatic
trace/relearning loop. Scope any cache by engine/source/team/rotation/target and
fitted region; do not persist it as a universally parsed mechanic. Persistent
mechanics IR and empirical local correction remain different kinds of knowledge.
